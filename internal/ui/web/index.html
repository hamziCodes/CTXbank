How CTXbank Protects Your Project (In 3 Simple Steps)

No tech jargon. Here is how CTXbank saves you time and prevents AI hallucinations.

1

The Memory Bank

CTXbank keeps 7 clean notes about your project (what you're building, your rules, what tech you use). When you chat with AI, it always remembers your guidelines.

2

The Save Button

Before letting AI write code or before switching tasks, save a Checkpoint. If something goes wrong, you can restore your exact previous state in 1 second.

3

Zero Token Waste

Normal tools force AI to re-read your whole project on every message. CTXbank only sends what changed (a tiny 72-byte check), saving you speed, tokens, and money.
